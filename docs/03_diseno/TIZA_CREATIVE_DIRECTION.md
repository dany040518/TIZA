# TIZA — Complete Creative Direction System

## The North Star

> *"TIZA doesn't automate teaching. It protects the human in the teacher."*

This distinction is foundational. Every design decision — from border radius to copy tone to animation easing — must serve this idea. TIZA is not productivity software. It is a sanctuary that happens to be powerful.

---

## 1. Brand Personality

TIZA has a personality, not just a visual identity. Think of TIZA as a character: a brilliant, quietly confident colleague who has already done the hard thinking before you arrive at your desk. She never lectures. She never crowds you. She simply knows.

**The five personality dimensions:**

**Serene intelligence** — TIZA is wise without being imposing. It doesn't flash its capability. It reveals it gently, when needed.

**Editorial authority** — TIZA has taste. The interface feels curated, not generated. Every element earns its place.

**Warm pragmatism** — TIZA understands that teachers are exhausted. It meets them there, without sentimentality, and clears the path.

**Quiet confidence** — No tooltips explaining how great it is. No feature announcements. Just substance.

**Human-first humility** — TIZA knows it supports teachers; it doesn't try to replace their judgment. It defers, always, to the educator.

**Brand voice keywords:** Calm. Clear. Considered. Precise. Warm. Never: chirpy, corporate, technical, urgent, gamified.

---

## 2. Emotional Design Principles

These are not aesthetic rules. They are emotional commitments the product makes to its user every session.

**Principle 1 — The Relief Principle**
The first sensation when opening TIZA must be relief. Not excitement, not stimulation — relief. Like setting down a heavy bag. White space, minimal visual noise, and a greeting that acknowledges context ("Good morning, Valentina. You have three things that actually need you today.") create this feeling immediately.

**Principle 2 — The Competence Mirror**
TIZA should make teachers feel more capable, not dependent. Every AI output must be framed as a starting point the teacher owns — never as a finished product they merely approve. This is not just ethical design; it's emotionally nourishing.

**Principle 3 — The No-Overwhelm Contract**
The interface never shows everything at once. Information is revealed progressively, based on intent and context. A teacher grading papers doesn't see attendance modules. A teacher planning lessons doesn't see reporting dashboards. The workspace morphs to the moment.

**Principle 4 — Invisible Labor Recognition**
Micro-copy and AI insights should occasionally acknowledge the invisible weight of teaching. Not patronizingly — but humanly. "You've created 47 lesson plans this semester" is more emotionally resonant than "Total plans: 47."

**Principle 5 — Calm as Aesthetic Stance**
Every color decision, spacing decision, and animation decision must pass the calm test. Does this increase cognitive load? Does this create urgency where none should exist? If yes — remove it.

## 3. UX Philosophy

**The Copilot Model, not the Dashboard Model**

Traditional education software gives teachers dashboards — grids of data waiting to be acted upon. TIZA inverts this. Instead of "here is your data," TIZA says "here is what matters today and why."

The four UX convictions:

**Contextual intelligence over feature access** — TIZA's navigation is not a list of features. It's a living workspace that reorganizes around the teacher's current context (time of day, schedule, pending deadlines, emotional load).

**Progressive disclosure as respect** — Showing everything is not helpfulness; it's aggression. TIZA shows one thing at a time, beautifully, with the confidence that the next thing will be there when needed.

**AI as texture, not overlay** — AI in TIZA is not a chatbot panel or a separate "AI Mode." It's woven into every interaction as a layer beneath the surface — surfacing when genuinely useful, invisible when not.

**Completion rituals** — When a teacher finishes a significant task (submits grades, completes a report, finalizes a lesson plan), TIZA marks the moment. Not with confetti — with stillness and acknowledgment. A quiet sense of closure.

## 4. Visual Language

The visual language of TIZA lives at the intersection of three rarely combined worlds:

- **Swiss editorial design** (structure, white space, typographic hierarchy)
- **Contemporary wellness apps** (warmth, breathing room, emotional resonance)
- **Luxury SaaS products** (restraint, precision, confidence)

**The four visual pillars:**

**1. Breathing Space**
Generous margins. Asymmetric layouts. Content never touches the edges of its container without intention. The page breathes. This is not emptiness — it is editorial authority.

**2. Tonal Warmth**
The white in TIZA is never cold. It leans slightly warm. The pink (#FCDDF1) is used sparingly — as an accent, a blush, a moment of softness in an otherwise monochrome world. The orange (#FA6B39) is reserved for energy: actions, live states, AI activity, moments of importance.

**3. Textural Contrast**
The interface combines hard geometry (crisp lines, precise grids) with soft moments (rounded AI output cards, gentle gradients on active states). This contrast creates emotional depth — the product feels both rigorous and approachable.

**4. Quiet Sophistication**
No gradients for decoration. No shadows for drama. No icons for everything. Only what is absolutely necessary — and that, done with exceptional craft.

**Color usage philosophy:**

- **White** — the dominant tone. Canvas, breathing room, clarity.
- **Black** — type, structure, authority. Used with confidence, not timidity.
- **Pink #FCDDF1** — warmth, femininity, softness. Used as background blushes, hover states on cards, subtle highlights on AI-generated content. Never used for critical actions.
- **Orange #FA6B39** — the active, alive color. Used for: the AI indicator, active navigation states, primary CTAs, progress moments, and the TIZA logomark accent. This is the heartbeat color.

## 5. Typography Direction

Typography is the primary design material in TIZA. It carries more personality than any illustration or icon set could.

**Type philosophy:** TIZA uses type as an editorial tool. Headlines are statements. Body text is precise. UI labels are invisible infrastructure.

**Recommended type system (pairing concept):**

**Display / Headlines:** A contemporary serif with editorial character — something in the lineage of Canela, Freight Display, or Editorial New. This brings warmth, intelligence, and a non-tech feel to a tech product. Used for: welcome messages, section headers, feature introductions, empty states.

**Interface / UI text:** A refined geometric sans-serif — something like Inter, Söhne, or Neue Haas Grotesk. Clean, highly legible at small sizes, neutral enough to not compete with the editorial serif. Used for: all functional UI, labels, data, forms.

**Monospace accents:** Used sparingly for data contexts — grades, counts, dates in grading/attendance views. Creates a quiet technical credibility without making the whole product feel technical.

**Typographic rules:**

- Headline sizes are large and confident — TIZA doesn't mumble
- Line heights are generous — text breathes
- Tracking on uppercase labels is open and deliberate
- No bold-everything approach — weight is used purposefully to create hierarchy, not noise
- Limit to three sizes per screen maximum — complexity is a failure of editing

## 6. Layout Philosophy

**The Intelligent Canvas**

TIZA's layouts follow a magazine logic, not an app grid logic. Content has weight and intentionality. Not every section is the same size. Not every card is the same importance.

**Layout convictions:**

**Asymmetry with structure** — Layouts are grid-based but deliberately asymmetric. A wide content zone and a narrower contextual zone. The AI panel doesn't live in a fixed sidebar — it appears as a floating, contextual layer.

**The Focus State** — When a teacher is in deep work (writing a lesson plan, grading, writing a report), TIZA enters a focus layout: the interface strips away everything except the task. Navigation collapses. The canvas expands. The teacher is alone with the work and TIZA.

**Vertical rhythm** — Consistent baseline grid. Everything lines up. Not rigidly, but harmonically — the way a well-set page of text feels right even if the reader can't articulate why.

**The Workspace Model** — The main interface is not tabbed. It's spatial. Different functional areas feel like different rooms in a thoughtfully designed space — each with its own light and character, but part of a coherent whole.

## 7. Component Style

TIZA's components are designed with the same philosophy as well-made objects: appropriate materials, honest construction, nothing unnecessary.

**Cards** — Low elevation. Subtle borders (1px, warm gray). Generous internal padding. Content-first — the card is a vessel, not the point. AI-generated cards have a distinct but quiet treatment: a thin pink-blush left border or a barely-there background wash.

**Buttons** — Two modes only: primary (solid black, white type, orange on hover/active) and ghost (transparent, black border). No button rainbow. No disabled-state clutter. If an action isn't available, it disappears — it doesn't grey out and remain visible.

**Forms** — Borderless field design. Underline inputs only, or full-bleed white fields with no visible border — only a warm shadow on focus. Labels animate out of the way. Error states use warm red, not alarm red. The form should feel like filling in a beautifully designed journal.

**Navigation** — Minimal. Icon + label. Left-positioned, collapsible. In mobile: a bottom dock with 4-5 maximum items. Never tabs within tabs. Never nested dropdowns.

**AI Output Components** — These deserve special attention. AI-generated content (lesson plan drafts, report summaries, recommendations) appears in a distinct container:
- Soft pink blush background
- A subtle orange left accent line
- The TIZA logo mark or a tiny "TIZA" label — never "AI generated" — framing the content
- Editable inline — the teacher can click any sentence and revise it
- A gentle fade-in animation — the content arrives, it doesn't pop

**Empty States** — Editorial illustrations (not cartoon characters) and a single sentence that is human, honest, and occasionally even poetic. "Nothing's due today. A rare gift." is better than "No pending tasks."

**Data visualizations** — Minimal. Line and bar only. Monochrome by default with orange as the single accent for the highlighted data point. No pie charts. No dashboard overload.

## 8. AI Interaction Philosophy

This is where TIZA must be most revolutionary.

**The TIZA AI Stance: Suggesting, Never Deciding**

TIZA's AI never announces itself with a fanfare. It is present as a layer of intelligence beneath the surface, surfacing when it has something genuinely worth saying.

**The three modes of AI presence:**

**Ambient intelligence** — AI is always reading context (current schedule, student data, upcoming deadlines) and pre-calculating what the teacher will need next. This is invisible and never communicated directly. The teacher simply experiences a product that seems to already know.

**Prompted partnership** — When a teacher starts a task (new lesson plan, new report), TIZA doesn't wait to be asked. It opens a pre-populated canvas: "Here's a starting point for Thursday's biology class based on your curriculum map and last week's session." The teacher refines, not creates from scratch.

**Conversational depth** — A natural language interface exists but is never the default. It's a power layer — accessed when the teacher wants to go deeper ("Show me which students are falling behind in comprehension") rather than navigate a feature.

**AI language rules:**
- Never says "I" — TIZA speaks in second person or passive. "A draft is ready" not "I've written a draft."
- Never explains its own process — no "I analyzed your data and determined..." Just the output, cleanly.
- Always gives the teacher the exit — every AI suggestion has a "discard and start fresh" option that is just as prominent as "use this."
- Acknowledges limits gracefully — "Not enough data to give a confident recommendation here" is honest and builds trust.

## 9. Motion and Animation Direction

**The Principle: Motion that Breathes**

Motion in TIZA is never decorative. It is informational and emotional — it communicates state, confirms action, and contributes to the calm feeling of the product.

**Motion vocabulary:**

**Transitions** — Fade + slight vertical shift (8-12px). Never slide-in from the side (too aggressive). Never pop (too jarring). Content arrives the way thoughts arrive — with gentle intention.

**AI loading states** — Not a spinner. A slow, rhythmic pulse on the orange accent — like a heartbeat at rest. This communicates activity without creating anxiety.

**Completion states** — When a task is submitted or completed, the element settles: a brief, satisfying scale-down (0.98) and a fade to a muted green or the TIZA pink blush. Quiet closure.

**Hover states** — Subtle. Background wash of pink blush (#FCDDF1 at 40% opacity). The card lifts slightly (translateY -2px). Nothing aggressive.

**Page transitions** — Cross-fade at 200-300ms. The product doesn't rush between states.

**Easing curves** — Exclusively ease-out for elements entering the screen (they arrive and settle). Ease-in for elements leaving (they withdraw politely). Never linear.

**What TIZA never does with motion:**
- Bounce
- Spin
- Flash
- Slide horizontally on mobile
- Animate data charts on load (this creates anxiety, not delight)

## 10. Landing Page Narrative

The landing page is not a product page. It is an editorial experience — a manifesto delivered with restraint.

**Narrative arc:**

**Act 1 — The Recognition** (above the fold)
The teacher sees themselves. Not a happy stock photo teacher. A real acknowledgment of their reality.

Headline options:
> *"Teaching is the most important work in the world. We built the tools to match it."*

> *"You became a teacher to teach. We'll handle the rest."*

> *"The intelligent workspace for educators who have given enough of themselves to administration."*

Visual: Full-width, editorial typography on white. A single orange accent. No hero image — just the words, large, confident, true.

**Act 2 — The Problem Articulated** (scroll 1)
In precise, empathetic language, TIZA names the pain. Not as a list of features to solve it — as a human observation about what's wrong with teaching today. Use data sparingly, but powerfully: "The average teacher spends 11 hours per week on administrative tasks. That's 440 hours per year not spent teaching."

**Act 3 — The Revelation** (scroll 2-3)
The product appears — not as screenshots, but as moments. A lesson plan assembling itself. A report completing. An insight surfacing. Short, ambient video loops. The teacher's experience, not the feature.

**Act 4 — The Philosophy** (scroll 4)
A brief, confident articulation of what TIZA believes. Not features. Beliefs.
> *"TIZA believes administrative systems should serve teachers, not the other way around."*
> *"TIZA believes AI should amplify human judgment, not replace it."*
> *"TIZA believes calm is a design value."*

**Act 5 — The Invitation** (scroll 5)
One CTA. Generous white space around it. No urgency language. No "Sign up free today!" Just:
> *"Request early access."* or *"Begin with TIZA."*

## 13. Complete Creative Direction System Summary

### The TIZA Design System — Core Tenets

**Identity:**
TIZA is the intelligent copilot for modern educators. It is calm, editorial, warm, and powerful. It never shouts. It always knows.

**Color system:**
- Background: Warm white (not #FFFFFF — use #FAFAF8 or similar)
- Surface: White #FFFFFF
- Primary text: Near-black (#0F0F0F)
- Secondary text: Warm gray (#6B6B6B)
- Accent warm: Pink #FCDDF1 (backgrounds, hover, AI content)
- Accent active: Orange #FA6B39 (CTAs, AI activity, active states, logomark)
- Border: #E8E5E0 (warm, not cold gray)

**Type system:**
- Display: Editorial serif (Canela, Editorial New, or similar)
- Interface: Geometric sans (Söhne, Inter, or Neue Haas Grotesk)
- Data: Monospace accent (for grades, dates, counts)

**Spacing:**
- Base unit: 8px
- Generous sections: 80-120px vertical breathing room
- Comfortable cards: 32px+ internal padding
- Column widths: Max 680px for reading content

**Voice:**
Precise. Warm. Confident. Human. Never urgent. Never technical. Never corporate.

**Motion:**
Fade + settle. Ease-out entries. Ease-in exits. Orange pulse for AI thinking. Quiet completions.

**AI philosophy:**
Ambient, woven, humble, editable, transparent about limits, always deferring to the teacher.

**The single design test:**
Before shipping any screen, ask: *Does a tired teacher at 7pm feel relieved or overwhelmed when they see this?*

If relieved — ship it.
If overwhelmed — go back.
